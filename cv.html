Professional Summary

Experienced in synthesis and characterization of electrode materials (carbon/aluminum) and separators (wood-based) for energy storage. Skilled in slurry preparation, electrode coating, coin/split/pouch cell fabrication, and test system setup.

Pioneered development of a thermally rechargeable supercapacitor technology (thermopower coefficient 100 mV/K) for low-grade heat energy harvesting.

Collaborative, multidisciplinary approach: extensive work on supercapacitors, nanoparticle synthesis, and biomedical imaging sensors.
