Basanta Ghimire

I am an electrochemical researcher and PhD candidate at Clemson University.
My work focuses on sustainable energy solutions and Nepali music.

Originally from Nepal, I came to the United States in 2019 to join Clemson University as a graduate student. My research centers on TReCO (Triboelectric Nanogenerators for Renewable Energy Conversion and Optimization), exploring innovative approaches to energy generation and storage.

Beyond my academic work, I maintain a deep passion for Nepali music and operate a YouTube channel where I share musical performances and compositions that celebrate my cultural heritage.
