About Me

Hello! I'm Basanta Ghimire, originally from Nepal, currently pursuing my PhD at Clemson University in South Carolina.

I came to the United States in 2019 to join Clemson as a graduate student in the field of electrochemical research.

My academic journey has been focused on advancing energy storage technologies, with my current work centered on TReCO (Triboelectric Nanogenerators for Renewable Energy Conversion and Optimization).

Beyond my research, I have a deep passion for Nepali music and maintain a YouTube channel where I share my musical creations.
