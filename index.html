About me
I’m an energy systems engineer working at the intersection of wet‑lab electrochemistry, materials characterization, and simulation. I synthesize and evaluate electrode and separator materials, build and test coin/split/pouch cells, and use LAMMPS/MATLAB/Python to design faster, more informative experiments. Recent highlights include the TRECO concept, wood‑based separators, and porous aluminum electrodes for enhanced surface area and performance.
Interests

• Thermal supercapacitors and low‑grade heat utilization
• Materials design and characterization (SEM, TEM, XRD, Raman)
• MD simulation (LAMMPS), data pipelines, and modeling
