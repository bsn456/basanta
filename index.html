ABOUT ME

I am a PhD scholar at Clemson University, working on cutting-edge energy harvesting devices and bioimaging sensors. My research is focused on developing innovative solutions for sustainable energy and advanced medical imaging technologies. The goal is to push the boundaries of what's possible in applied physics and engineering.

My foundational engineering knowledge was built during my undergraduate studies at St. Xavier's College, Kathmandu, which prepared me for the rigors of advanced research.

RESEARCH

CURRENT PROJECTS

My work at Clemson University is primarily focused on two key areas:

  • **Energy Harvesting:** Developing novel, high-efficiency piezoelectric nanogenerators for powering small-scale electronics and wireless sensors. This involves material synthesis, device fabrication, and performance testing.
  • **Bioimaging Sensors:** Designing and fabricating advanced fluorescent sensors for real-time, non-invasive imaging of biological processes at the cellular level. This has applications in medical diagnostics and drug discovery.
